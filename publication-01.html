The role of extracted free chains in adhesion on soft gel surfaces可提取自由链在软凝胶表面黏附中的作用

Wenjie Qian, Keli Zhang, Qin Xu

Langmuir 42(31), 22623–22629 (2026)

Abstract摘要

Adhesion on soft polymeric materials involves large deformations and transport within the polymer network. Using dual-wavelength confocal microscopy, this work characterizes contact-induced extraction of free chains and the adhesion profiles of microspheres placed on silicone gels with different crosslinking densities. The measurements reveal a locally phase-separated region near the contact while the surface-stress balance remains well defined. At the global scale, indentation depth and contact radius follow the competition between adhesion energy and surface stress captured by classical contact models. A slow increase in indentation over time further points to a gradual rise in adhesion energy on a poroelastic diffusion timescale.

软聚合物材料的黏附伴随着大变形以及聚合物网络内部的物质迁移。本研究利用双波长共聚焦显微镜，研究不同交联密度硅凝胶上微球接触引起的自由链提取与黏附形貌。结果显示，接触区域附近会形成局部相分离区，同时表面应力平衡仍然清晰可辨；在宏观尺度上，压入深度和接触半径由黏附能与表面应力之间的竞争决定。压入深度随时间缓慢增加，说明黏附能会在孔弹性扩散时间尺度上逐步增长。
