About关于我

I am a researcher in soft materials and interfacial mechanics. My work focuses on how liquids interact with compliant polymer networks, how surface structures emerge and evolve, and how microscopic material organization controls macroscopic adhesion and deformation.

I combine carefully controlled experiments with quantitative imaging and mechanics-based analysis. My research has addressed wetting-induced phase separation on soft solids, adhesion on gel surfaces, polymer-coating fracture, microscopy-lens distortion correction, and micro-scale digital image correlation.

I received my Ph.D. in Nano Science and Technology from the Department of Physics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Qin Xu. Before that, I studied mechanics at Jiangsu University and civil engineering at Qingdao University of Technology.

我的研究聚焦于软物质与界面力学，关注液体如何与柔性聚合物网络相互作用、表面结构如何产生与演化，以及微观材料组织如何影响宏观黏附和变形行为。

我将精细可控实验、定量成像与基于力学的分析方法相结合。相关研究包括软固体表面的润湿诱导相分离、凝胶表面黏附、聚合物涂层断裂、显微镜头畸变校正以及微尺度数字图像相关方法。

我在香港科技大学物理系获得纳米科学与技术博士学位，导师为许钦教授。此前，我分别在江苏大学学习力学、在青岛理工大学学习土木工程。
