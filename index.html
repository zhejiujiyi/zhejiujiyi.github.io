Interfaces · Optical mechanics · Simulation界面 · 光测力学 · 仿真

Wenjie Qian 钱文杰

I study interfacial mechanics across soft materials, polymer coatings, and engineering systems. My work combines non-contact optical measurement with mechanics-based modelling to understand wetting, adhesion, phase separation, deformation, and fracture.

My methods include digital image correlation, shearography, confocal and white-light interferometric imaging, photolithography, finite-element analysis, and multiphysics simulation.

我的研究聚焦于软物质、聚合物涂层与工程系统中的界面力学问题，将非接触式光学测量与力学建模相结合，研究润湿、黏附、相分离、变形和断裂。

主要方法包括数字图像相关、剪切散斑干涉、激光共聚焦与白光干涉成像、光刻、有限元分析及多物理场仿真。
