Selected themes主要方向

Research研究方向

My research combines soft-matter physics, experimental mechanics, and quantitative imaging to understand material behavior across scales.我的研究结合软物质物理、实验力学与定量成像，理解跨尺度的材料行为。

Soft matter软物质

Wetting-induced phase separation润湿诱导相分离

Liquids can reorganize a soft polymer network and create distinct surface domains. I study how these domains emerge, grow, and interact with the mechanical properties of the underlying solid.

液体能够重组软聚合物网络并形成不同的表面结构域。我关注这些结构域如何产生、生长，以及它们如何与基底固体的力学性质相互作用。

Interfaces界面

Adhesion on soft gel surfaces软凝胶表面黏附

Soft-gel adhesion is shaped by network structure, surface composition, and extractable free chains. This work seeks mechanistic links between microscopic organization and measurable adhesive response.

软凝胶的黏附行为受到网络结构、表面组成与可提取自由链的共同影响。相关工作旨在建立微观组织与可测黏附响应之间的机制联系。

Methods and applications方法与应用

Digital image correlation数字图像相关

Micro-scale displacement and strain measurement using calibrated speckle imaging.利用经标定的散斑成像实现微尺度位移与应变测量。

Optical calibration光学校正

Lens-distortion correction for microscopy systems used in quantitative deformation measurements.针对定量变形测量显微系统的镜头畸变校正。

Coating mechanics涂层力学

Fracture toughness, interfacial bonding, residual stress, and mechanical characterization.断裂韧度、界面结合、残余应力与力学性能表征。

Engineering simulation工程仿真

Mechanics-based modelling that connects experimental insight with material and product development.以力学建模连接实验认识与材料、产品研发。
